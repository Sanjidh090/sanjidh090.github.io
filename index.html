Sanjid Hasan

KUET, Khulna-9203, Bangladesh

I am a Electrical & Electronic engineering student at KUET working at the intersection of speech intelligence and efficient inferences. My research focuses on developing compact, robust speech-recognition systems for Bangla and building accessible technologies for speakers underserved by mainstream recognition systems, including people with dysarthria.

Research interests:

  1. Edge-efficient ASR — making compact recognisers like Moonshine and whistle work outside English.
  2. Accessible speech — streaming recognition for dysarthric speakers, running on a CPU.
  3. Low-resource Bangla NLP — corpora, diarization and fine-tuning for a language of 300 million speakers.
  4. Generative Computer Vision — World models, GANs, diffusion models

Tokenizer Transplantation: Mitigating Autoregressive Collapse in Edge-Efficient Bengali ASR
Sanjid Hasan, Md. Abdur Rahman · July 2026
Moonshine breaks down on Bengali because its English byte-level tokenizer shatters each word. Replacing the decoder vocabulary with BanglaBERT's WordPiece removes the instability and cuts decoding by 85.8%.
